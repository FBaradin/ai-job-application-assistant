Senior Business Intelligence Analyst - Northwind Freight (Hybrid, Miami FL)

About us
Northwind Freight is a mid-size third-party logistics provider moving temperature-controlled goods across the Southeast.

What you'll do
- Own the reporting layer for our transportation and warehouse operations teams.
- Design, build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau that leadership uses to run weekly business reviews.
- Write and optimise SQL against our Snowflake data warehouse; build transformation models in dbt.
- Partner with finance on freight-cost analysis, carrier scorecards and accrual reconciliation.
- Translate ambiguous business questions into clear analyses and present findings to non-technical audiences.
- Mentor two junior analysts and set reporting standards for the team.

What you bring
- 5+ years in analytics or BI, ideally in logistics, supply chain or transportation.
- Expert SQL; working knowledge of Python for automation.
- Hands-on experience with Power BI (Tableau a plus).
- Experience with cloud data warehouses (Snowflake preferred) and dbt.
- Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills.
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field.

Nice to have
- Experience with TMS/WMS data (e.g. MercuryGate, Manhattan).
- Spanish language skills.
